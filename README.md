ITS WORK TIME

Learn. Practise. Experience. Progress.

ITS WORK TIME is a global professional simulation universe designed to allow people to explore, learn and practise different professions through immersive 3D environments and interactive multiplayer experiences.

Our Vision

To create a connected virtual world where people can experience professional careers, develop knowledge, practise decision-making and progress through realistic simulations.

First Department: Nursing

The first development phase focuses on nursing education and professional simulation.

Initial Features

* Country-based registration
* Student profiles and virtual identities
* Nursing career progression
* First-person 3D hospital environment
* Interactive clinical scenarios
* AI-controlled virtual patients
* Professional training mode
* Nursing life simulation mode
* Multiplayer teamwork

Future Departments

* Medicine
* Pharmacy
* Aviation
* Engineering
* Military
* Law enforcement
* Other professional fields

Technology Direction

* PlayCanvas
* JavaScript
* Blender
* Supabase
* Colyseus
* AI-assisted development tools

Development Philosophy

Build progressively, test carefully and prioritise accessibility across ordinary devices.

Clinical simulations will be reviewed by qualified educators and will complement, not replace, supervised professional training.
